About Habesha Eatery

Habesha Eatery is dedicated to bringing the rich flavors of Ethiopia to our guests. We prepare every dish using fresh, locally sourced ingredients and authentic Ethiopian spices. Our goal is to provide an unforgettable dining experience where tradition, hospitality, and delicious food come together.

Whether you're enjoying a family dinner, celebrating a special occasion, or trying Ethiopian cuisine for the first time, our welcoming atmosphere makes every visit memorable.

Our Signature Dishes

  • Doro Wat — Slow-simmered chicken in berbere and seasoned butter; Ethiopia’s national dish..
  • Tibs — Tender beef or lamb sautéed with onions, garlic, herbs, and aromatic spices.
  • Shiro Wat — Velvety chickpea stew, smooth, comforting, and full of flavor.
  • Misir Wat — Red lentils cooked in fragrant berbere sauce.
  • Kitfo — Minced beef seasoned with mitmita and warm spiced butter, served raw or cooked.

Our Mission

Our mission is to preserve and share Ethiopia's rich culinary heritage by serving authentic meals prepared with passion, quality ingredients, and exceptional customer service.
